Privacy Policy

Papillon Image™ respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information collected through this website and during enquiries or bookings may be used and protected.

Information we may collect

When you contact Papillon Image™, make an enquiry or book a service, we may collect information such as your:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • contact number;
  • information provided in an enquiry or contact form; and
  • information reasonably required to provide the service you have requested.

How your information is used

Your personal information may be used to:

  • respond to enquiries;
  • communicate with you about consultations, bookings or services;
  • provide the services you have requested;
  • process and maintain appropriate business records; and
  • meet applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Papillon Image™ will not sell your personal information.

Sharing of information

Personal information will only be shared where reasonably necessary to operate the website or provide services, where required by law, or where you have authorised us to do so.

Where third-party service providers are used, reasonable steps will be taken to ensure that personal information is handled appropriately.

Protecting your information

Reasonable measures are taken to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure or alteration.

Personal information will only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law.

Cookies and website analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality and understand how visitors use the site.

Where applicable, your browser settings can be used to manage or disable cookies.

Your rights

In accordance with applicable South African data protection legislation, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may request access to personal information held about you or ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.

Where applicable, you may also object to certain processing or request that personal information be deleted, subject to legal and legitimate business requirements.

Contact

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, please contact:

Papillon Image™

Email: helga@papillon-image.co.za

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, business practices or applicable legal requirements.
