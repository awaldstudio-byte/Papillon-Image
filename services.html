Common questions

A little clarity before you begin.

Where is the best place to start?

KALEIDOSCOPE™ — Colour Analysis is the best place to start. It identifies the colours that naturally enhance your complexion, brighten your eyes and bring your natural beauty to life.

Do Colour and Style consultations include the Pocket Stylist App?

Yes. Colour and Style consultations include lifetime access to your personalised Pocket Stylist guidance — COLOUR for KALEIDOSCOPE™ and STYLE for SIGNATURE STYLE™.

Can I book Colour and Style together?

Yes. The complete KALEIDOSCOPE™ + SIGNATURE STYLE™ Colour & Style Experience is R2,800.

How long does a Wardrobe Edit take?

Every wardrobe is different. THE WARDROBE EDIT™ is R350 per hour with a minimum of 3 hours, and the time required depends on the size of your wardrobe and what you would like to achieve.

How does personal shopping work?

SHOP WITH PURPOSE™ is tailored to your individual needs and personalised shopping list. You meet Helga at an agreed shopping centre. The service is R350 per hour with a minimum of 3 hours.

Do you offer group experiences, workshops or talks?

Yes. Papillon Image offers selected consultations, masterclasses and workshops for groups, as well as tailored workshops and women’s talks for teams, organisations and events.

What about travel costs?

Where a consultation or service requires travel, any applicable travel costs will be discussed and quoted in advance.
